# RBI Circular (Illustrative) — Periodic Updation of KYC

> NOTE: This is a SYNTHETIC sample modelled loosely on the style of an RBI
> master circular, for demonstrating the RAG pipeline. It is NOT a real RBI
> document and must not be relied on.

**Circular No.:** RBI/2025-26/SAMPLE-114
**Date:** 02 May 2026
**To:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

## 1. Background
In terms of the Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC), regulated entities
are required to carry out periodic updation of KYC records of existing customers
on a risk-based approach.

## 2. Periodicity of Updation
- **High-risk customers:** at least once every **2 years**.
- **Medium-risk customers:** at least once every **8 years**.
- **Low-risk customers:** at least once every **10 years**.

## 3. No-Change Declaration
Where there is no change in KYC information, a self-declaration to that effect
may be obtained from the customer through the customer's registered email,
registered mobile number, ATMs, digital channels (such as net banking / mobile
application), or letter.

## 4. Updation of Address
Where there is a change only in the address, the customer may submit a revised
address via the channels in Section 3. The bank shall verify the declared
address within **two months**.

## 5. Account Operation
Banks shall not impose restrictions on the operation of accounts solely for the
reason that periodic updation is pending, unless required under a specific
regulatory or statutory directive. Customers shall be given prior intimation of
at least **three** advance notices, including at least one by letter.

## 6. Effective Date
This circular is effective from the date of issue.
